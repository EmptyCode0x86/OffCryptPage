Community & Support

Official Discord for product help and coding discussion

The official OffCode Discord is where you can get product support and talk about coding around OffCode tools. Joining is optional. The software does not require a Discord account.

Use #support for issues, installation, and feature requests. Use the coding channel for general programming discussion. For license activation and GDPR data-rights requests, email support@dev-offcode.com.

#support

Ask about OffCrypt, Screen Lock Builder, Automation Tool, Web Remote Control, and Tor Chat. Installation questions, bugs, and feature requests belong here.

Coding

General programming discussion related to OffCode projects. Keep product bugs and install help in #support so they stay easy to find.

To get help faster, describe the problem clearly and share error messages or screenshots when they are relevant. We aim to respond as quickly as possible. Bigger bugs may take longer.

Discord is a third-party service. Joining requires a Discord account. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. Discord Inc. processes account data, IP addresses, and messages under the Discord Privacy Policy. We do not send license HWID or product telemetry to Discord. You do not need Discord to use OffCode software. Privacy and data-rights requests still go to support@dev-offcode.com.
